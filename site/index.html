Tideway Trainer

Row from the Furnivall Sculling Club pontoon on the real Thames, among other crews, and get off at the pontoon when you are done. The UL Boat House is optional – reach it for a bonus.

The row: you start alongside the Furnivall Sculling Club pontoon (Middlesex bank, just above Hammersmith Bridge) and row where you like. Rowing as far as the UL Boat House (past Hammersmith, Chiswick Eyot, Barnes and Chiswick Bridge) earns a bonus. Whenever you are back at the pontoon (green/gold ring, keep to the Middlesex side) after rowing away from it, a Get off the boat button appears – press it to finish and record your score.

Other crews: single sculls to eights are on the river, some sitting still and some rowing slowly. They always obey the Code (stationary and against-the-stream boats in the Inshore Zone, boats with the stream on the starboard side of the Fairway). Overtake them on the outside (river-centre side) with room to spare, never within 75 m of a bridge and never into oncoming traffic: that is a clean overtake (+100). Anything else is a dodgy overtake (−100, +5 s). Hitting a boat is +15 s. In auto-steer the boat goes round them for you; use Space (easy oar) to hold back behind a slower boat near a bridge.
The coach: break the Code, collide, run aground or overtake badly and the coach launch roars up alongside to shout at you.

Left/right are relative to the direction of travel. Lanes are fixed in the high-water river: left/right = the Inshore Zones, middle = the starboard (right-hand) half of the Fairway. At low tide an Inshore Zone can dry out – the auto-steered lane then follows the water's edge; in manual steering (←/→) you can run aground. The tidal stream is strongest in the deep water on the outside of bends and weakest inshore.

Tideway Code (PLA, Upper Tideway Code Area): rowing against the stream, work the slacks in the Inshore Zone – Surrey bank from Hammersmith to the Chiswick Steps Crossing Zone, Middlesex bank from there to the UL Boat House – and only cross at the Crossing Zones. Rowing with the stream (or at slack water), keep to the starboard side of the Fairway. Out of position for more than 4 s = +5 s penalty. Hitting a bridge pier, a pier/pontoon or grounding = +15 s. Bridge arches are numbered #1 (Middlesex) to #3 (Surrey); pier positions are approximate.
Turning round: press U to sweep across the river and head back. The Code says never to spin on the spot, so the boat arcs across the river – it needs room, and the tide carries you while you turn, so don't turn just upstream of a bridge or pier. Past the end of the map the boat turns round by itself.
